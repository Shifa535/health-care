# Health-care
healthcare data analysis using python and visualisation.
## Data cleaning 
Missing values,Duplicate values,Incorrect data types,Column name.
## Objective
To study the relationship between pollutants and AQI,
To compare  air quality across different regions,
To identify the major pollutants affecting AQI.
## Conclusion
This project analyzed the factors affecting the Air Quality diffIndex (AQI). The univariate, bivariate,and group analyses showed that air quality varies across different regions and pollution levels.Pollutants such as PM2.5,PM10,NO2,and CO have a strong impact an AQI. Some regions showed higher AQI values, indicating poor air quality. The analysis helps in understanding air pollution and supports better environmental and public health decisions.
## Suggestion
industries should follow strict pollution control regulations, and regular inspections should be conducted.
public awareness programs should be organized to educate people about the health effects of air pollution.
the use of clean and renewable energy source should be promoted to reduce dependence on fossil fuels.
